02

Live · Active development

TaxPrep AU (Xoba Paycheck)

A privacy-focused Australian pay and tax-preparation application. It reads supported payslips and statements, tracks pay, withholding and recorded super, checks financial-year completeness, prepares evidence handovers and protects user-controlled backups with browser WebCrypto. Unsupported payslip layouts can use an optional, rate-limited assisted reader before the user confirms every figure.
